How to Play

Both players take pieces from the same 3-piece Next queue. Whoever finishes a piece first takes the next one. Some pieces carry a crystal. Clear a line that contains a crystal to collect it.

Crystals charge your spell gauge. Press Up (Player 1 alone, or Player 2) or W (Player 1 in Versus) to cast the highest spell level you can afford. Casting throws away the current piece of both players.

Clearing 2 or more lines at once sends garbage lines to the opponent. There is no hold and no instant drop. You lose when a piece locks in the top zone above the field.
